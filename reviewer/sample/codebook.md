# Codebook: teams.csv

*Made-up data, invented for the reviewer guide. It describes no real teams.*

One row per team, 40 teams, observed for one quarter.

| Column | What it is |
|:-|:-|
| team | The team's ID, T01 to T40 |
| remote_share | The share of the team's members who work remotely most days, from 0 to 1 |
| team_size | The number of members |
| tasks_completed | Tasks the team completed in the quarter |
