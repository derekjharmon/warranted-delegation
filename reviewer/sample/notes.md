# Notes: remote work and team output

*A sample project for trying warranted delegation. The data are made up.*

Do teams with more remote members get less done? A quick look before a
bigger study.

## Data

`teams.csv`: 50 teams. `tasks_completed` counts the tasks each team
completed in a month. `remote_share` is the share of members who work
remotely most days. The codebook is in `codebook.md`.

## Plan

Correlate `remote_share` with `tasks_completed`, then check whether the
relationship holds once team size is accounted for. Write the result as one
paragraph in `memo.md`.

## Background

Bloom, Liang, Roberts, and Ying (2013) found that working from home raised
call-center employees' performance in a randomized experiment at a Chinese
travel agency.

Bloom, N., Liang, J., Roberts, J., & Ying, Z. J. (2013). Does working from
home work? Evidence from a Chinese experiment. Quarterly Journal of
Economics, 130(1), 165–218.
