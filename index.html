The two tests of the reader

The reader was tested twice against preregistered answer keys, on simulated authors whose every move was scripted, so the right codes were known in advance (Appendix A5).

Second test, the fixed reader. Simulated authors carried out three small projects, one author in each of the four cells (high or low delegation, crossed with high or low warrant), for twelve runs and 725 units in the key. The preregistered bar was κ of 0.6 on three codes: whether the author made each call or handed it over, how each handed-over call ended, and how each claim was checked. The reader cleared the bar on all three, with κ of 0.71 to 0.78, both through the API and on an author's own subscription. It found 94% to 96% of the key's units.
