## Background

Tobin (1969) argued that firms invest when the market values their capital
above what it would cost to replace it, a ratio now called q. Hayashi (1983)
showed that marginal q and average q are never equal, so the q we can observe
cannot guide investment.

## Data

The data come from Yehuda Grunfeld's 1958 doctoral thesis at Harvard and are
described in detail by Kleiber and Zeileis (2010). They cover large US
corporations from 1935 to 1954. Mean annual investment across the ten firms
is 155.9.

## Methods

We estimate the model by pooled OLS with heteroskedasticity-robust standard
errors.
