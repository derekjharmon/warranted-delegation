  • Given vs taken falls below 0.6. Under PLAN's Deviations item 9, the disclosures report "handed over" without the split.
  • "No check" vs "wasn't notified" can't be estimated yet. The key has only one claim coded "wasn't notified," so κ is 0 even at 98% agreement. The category stays in the codebook, but it is unvalidated until runs produce more cases.

Listing. The reader's quotes overlapped 175 of the key's 205 units (85%). Most of the other 30 it did list, but it quoted a different spot (for example, the interpretation anchored on "in years when a firm's market value is 10 percent higher" instead of "The results support the hypothesis"), so the pre-registered overlap rule doesn't pair them. The reader also lists at a finer grain, especially numbers in the text: it listed 282 units against the key's 205.

Sensitivity check, not pre-registered. Matching by checklist row first, then by overlap, pairs 171 units, with κ of 0.74, 0.75, and 0.79 on the primary codes, and 0.55 for given vs taken. The conclusion doesn't depend on the matching rule.

Cost: $3.21 of API usage for all four runs (14 model calls, about 14 minutes); about $3.30 per full pass across three runs.

The two measures move independently, as the 2×2 predicts:

  • Direction splits careful and unchecked (little handed over) from careless and owned (most handed over).
  • Follow-up splits careful and owned (taken back and checked) from unchecked and careless (neither).

The finished memos can't show this. The careless memo is as correct as the careful one, and the unchecked memo, the most tightly directed, is the one that kept all five planted errors.
