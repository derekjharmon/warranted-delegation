The runs and the key

  • Twelve runs, three tasks in four cells each, about 30 minutes in all; every record verifies. The other author's skill was used and captured in every run, and both brainstorm segments were labeled where the scripts put them.
  • The key: 725 units (350 decisions, 375 claims), including 209 judgment-certification units against the target of about 160. 390 planted, 324 labeled by Claude under a first-test ruling, 11 new kinds; 29 overrides where Claude departed from a script, most of them checks that by the record never read the source.
  • The author's part: he ruled on the 11 new kinds, agreeing with Claude's code on 7, and spot-checked 33 of the 325 labels applied by precedent (a random tenth, seed 20261017), changing none (labels/author.md, labels/spotcheck.md).
  • The planted errors: of the 56 that were errors in their run (error 5 was no error in four high-delegation runs, where the description matched the code), 50 were caught by the checks or fixed by Claude unasked. Six survived: all five in RAND low-delegation, low-warrant, and one in RAND high-delegation, low-warrant. In most low-warrant runs Claude fixed the errors unasked, as in the first test, so only the disclosure tells those runs apart from careful ones. Each run's fates are in labels/notes/.

Cost: $19.37 on the API key for the API pass (85 minutes of model time); the headless pass billed nothing extra ($31.90 at list prices, on the subscription; 90 minutes).

Departures from the preregistration

  1. Seven runs first refused to start. Claude's adapter work briefly changed the attested plugin folder while the runs were queued, and each run's start-up check refused, before making a project or running anything. They were requeued once the folder was clean and finished normally.
  2. labels/tools.py gained a check command (it validates label files) and a Claude, new kind value (so new kinds stay out of the spot-check draw) after the attest. Neither changes the procedure.
  3. score.py reads files with undecodable bytes replaced (text_at), changed after the reading and before any score was seen, when a reader unit naming a PNG figure stopped the scorer. A quote can't match inside an image either way; nothing changes for text files.
  4. The agreement key's summary line miscounts its low-high planted decisions (it says 9 kept and 8 handed over; its table, which the labels follow, gives 10 and 7).

Before Claude's labels were committed, two of them were made consistent across the six labeling agents (noted in the runs' notes): "what counts as blank" was marked a new kind in all four agreement runs, and two RAND methods descriptions became "no check" to match the first test's ruling.

Limits

Claude did the runs, the reading, and most of the labeling; the author ruled on the new kinds and spot-checked a tenth, and the "Claude graded Claude" limit is larger than in the first test. The simulated author's lines are scripted, the tasks are small, and two of the three are quantitative. Current models take no temperature setting, so a rerun would vary.
